# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone.

Be constructive, assume good faith, critique ideas rather than people, disclose conflicts of interest, and do not publish private information. Harassment, threats, discrimination, or deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Enforcement decisions should protect affected people and minimize unnecessary disclosure.
